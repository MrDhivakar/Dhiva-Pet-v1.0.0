## ✨ What is Dhiva Pet?

Dhiva Pet is a playful desktop companion for Windows. Watch 11 unique characters walk, jump, fly, swing, and fight across your screen — always on top, always local, never intrusive.

**100% local. No ads. No tracking. No accounts.**

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ **Transparent overlay** — Sits above all your windows
- ✅ **Smart physics** — Gravity, jumping, falling, landing
- ✅ **Rope swing & web-slinging** — Real physics simulations
- ✅ **Flying heroes** — Iron Man, Thor, Dr. Doom fly above
- ✅ **Signature attacks** — Repulsors, shields, lightning, AOE
- ✅ **Fight mode** — Characters battle each other
- ✅ **Multi-monitor** — Works across all displays
- ✅ **System tray** — Control from anywhere
- ✅ **Start with Windows** — Auto-launch option
- ✅ **100% offline** — Zero network requests
